## 1.0.0

- First version in the ShareOS app store: 16-step sequencer (kick, snare, hi-hat, clap, synth bass) with presets and mute per track, synthesizer with keyboard, oscilloscope.
- Look-ahead scheduling on the audio clock for exact timing; BPM and volume change live.
- WAV export (1–8 bars, stereo) rendered offline with the same sound engine, loops without a gap; download, save on the box, or send to WinWeb Player.
- Projects as `.beatbox` files: open and save on the box (permission `files`), opens from the file browser.
- ShareOS look (light/dark, accent colour); storage per account.
